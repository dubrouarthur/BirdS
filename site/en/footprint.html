Method and sources

  • Bird S 4B, electricity. Upper-bound estimate per short request: 72 W (GPU board power) × up to 5.5 s of compute × PUE 1.15, i.e. at most 0.13 Wh; about 0.04 Wh for the briefest requests. The in-app meter applies the same method to every answer.
  • Bird S 4B, water. 0.13 Wh × 0.15 L/kWh, the water usage effectiveness published by the cloud provider: about 0.02 mL. Estimate.
  • ChatGPT. Average query: about 0.34 Wh and 0.32 mL (Sam Altman, “The Gentle Singularity”, June 2025).
  • Gemini. Median text prompt: 0.24 Wh and 0.26 mL (Google, August 2025, arxiv.org/abs/2508.15734).
  • Ratios. 0.34 / 0.13 ≈ 2.6 and 0.34 / 0.04 ≈ 8.5: up to 8 times less electricity than an average ChatGPT query. 0.32 / 0.02 = 16: about 15 times less water. The ratios apply to ChatGPT only; Gemini is shown for reference. These figures come from different methods and scopes.
  • Size and compute. 4 billion parameters against about 400 billion for a large dense model: about 100 times smaller, and up to 100 times less compute per generated word, in theory.
  • Goals. Low-carbon data centres, 100 % renewable by 2030, smaller models and a local version are goals, not the current state.
