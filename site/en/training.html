A far lower cost

Small model, small bill.

Training a giant takes enormous computing power. Bird, about 100 times smaller than a large dense model, trains at a far lower cost.

  • Fewer parameters to tune.

    Each example takes far less computation.

  • Short training runs.

    You see results quickly, and adjust without waiting.

  • Retraining costs next to nothing.

    A procedure changes? Bird updates, without starting from scratch.

  • Frugal, end to end.

    A lean model in training as in use.

Your data

Your data stays under your control.

  • You choose the examples. Nothing goes into training without your agreement.
  • They serve your version of Bird. Your examples train your model, and nothing else.
  • Your version is yours alone. What Bird learns with you stays with you.
