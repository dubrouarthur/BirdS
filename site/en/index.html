Why small

Small is smarter.

  • Frugal.

    A hundred times smaller than a large dense model: less compute for every word and, per short request, over 4 times less electricity than an average ChatGPT query.

  • Light.

    Idle when nobody uses it: no compute when there is nothing to do.

  • Transparent.

    Under every answer, Bird shows its tokens, its compute time and the estimated energy. Bird shows you what each answer cost.

Sources and method

  1. Size: Bird S 4B has 4 billion parameters; a large dense model has about 400 billion. In theory, compute per generated word follows the number of parameters.
  2. Short request: 2,000 tokens read (instructions, tools and question included) and 500 tokens written. Electricity estimated from tokens, like the in-app meter: 2 × 0.006 Wh + 0.5 × 0.10 Wh = 0.062 Wh, rounded up to 0.07 Wh, whole service included (per-token values from published measurements: ML.ENERGY Benchmark, How Hungry is AI?; scope: Google, August 2025). Water: 0.07 Wh × 0.15 L of cooling water per kWh = 0.0105 mL.
  3. Average ChatGPT query: about 0.34 Wh and 0.32 mL (Sam Altman, “The Gentle Singularity”, June 2025). 0.34 / 0.07 ≈ 4.9 and 0.32 / 0.0105 ≈ 30. Full method
