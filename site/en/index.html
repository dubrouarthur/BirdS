Why small

Small is smarter.

  • Frugal.

    A hundred times smaller than a large dense model: less compute for every word and, per short request, up to 8 times less electricity than an average ChatGPT query.

  • Light.

    The server goes to sleep when nobody uses it, and Reasoning mode stays off until you turn it on.

  • Transparent.

    Under every answer, Bird shows its tokens, its compute time and the estimated energy. Bird shows you what each answer cost.
