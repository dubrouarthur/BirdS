Our approach

Small, on purpose.

The industry grows by reflex. We shrink by choice: the smallest AI that truly does the job.

  • The right size
  • Built for office work
  • Tested
  • Frugal by default
  • You stay in control

01The right size

Start from the work, not the size.

Our days are made of precise tasks: answering an email, preparing five slides, putting an invoice into a table, finding a source.

None of that needs a giant. With 4 billion parameters, Bird S 4B is built for this work, in French and in English.

02Built for office work

Cut out for office work.

Instead of aiming at everything, we train Bird on what you do every day: emails, minutes, decks, spreadsheets, research. A model that knows exactly what is expected of it does not need to be a giant.

  • 4 Bparameters
  • FR · ENbilingual
  • OfficeWord, Excel, PowerPoint

Train Bird on your tasks →

03Tested, case by case

Put to the test, case by case.

Tested on our internal test bench: tool calls, files, language, resistance to traps.

04Frugal by default

The thrifty setting is the factory setting.

  • 4 billion parameters: less compute for every word.
  • Idle when nobody uses it: no wasted compute.
  • Every answer shows its tokens, its time and its estimated energy.

05You stay in control

Bird proposes. You decide.

  • It drafts your emails and never sends them on your behalf.
  • It spots hidden instructions in a web page and asks you before acting.
  • It cites its sources and shows its steps.
  • Your conversations stay in your browser.
